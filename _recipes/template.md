## Ingredients

### For the noodles

- [ ] Dry spaghetti noodles, 0.5kg
- [ ] Water, 3L

### For the sauce

- [ ] Olive oil _(Not extra virgin)_, 50g
- [ ] Tomatoes 4 firm, 300g
- [ ] Basil 10 leaves, 15g
- [ ] Garlic, 3 cloves, 20g
- [ ] Pepper, _lots_

## Instructions

### Make the sauce

1. Heat up olive oil
2. Crush then add tomatoes
3. Muddle then add the basil
4. Add the garlic
5. Work up gumption
6. Add pepper

### Make the noodles

1. Heat up water
2. Add noodles
3. Cook em al dente
4. Drain, do not rinse
5. Add to sauce

### Serve forth

1. Spoon some into bowl
2. Serve with spoon and fork
3. Deliver with love
