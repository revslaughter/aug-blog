# Some Tips for Healthy Mums

FREE Gardening Workshop at Antioch Urban Growers

Mums thrive in cool weather, so wait for a week when the forecast calls for
temperatures in the 60s or low 70s before making your purchase. Higher
temperatures can cause them to bloom quickly, but the blooms won't last nearly
as long. If you can't wait, consider moving them to a cool space, either indoors
or somewhere well shaded during the hottest part of the day.

If you love the look of your mums, and want to enjoy them longer, consider
purchasing and planting hardy mums. Mums have a better chance of returning if
they are planted in the Spring, but mums can be planted in the Fall.

## Purchasing Fall Mums

- Check the moisture in the soil.
- Dry as a bone, this mum hasn't been watered.
- Very wet and soggy, overwatered,.
- Moist enough for a few particles to cling to your skin - Perfect!
- Bruised, bent, or broken stems = pick another.

## Success For Potted Mums

- Soak in a few inches of water in a bucket for about 20-60 min. Mums drink from
  the roots up
- Re-pot your mum, after soaking it. Loosen & trim any long, tangled roots, add
  compost-rich soil
- Place in the sunniest spot you can find (at least six hours of sun a day)

## Watering Mums

Mums drink from the roots up. When watering your mum, it's best to water it from
the roots. Avoid splashing or getting any water on the leaves. For potted mums,
place a tray or plate under the pot and keep it filled with water. For mums in
the ground, soaking the ground around the plant is best. Under-Watering Signs:
Wilting in the morning, dry soil below the surface, and bud drop. Over-Watering
Signs: Yellowing lower leaves, soft stems at soil level, and still-wet soil two
days after watering.

## Keeping Mums For More Than One Season

You can bring your mums inside for the winter after the first hard frost. Place
them in a cool dark place like your basement or attached garage. Water is
easiest if you simply place the pots in a shallow water pan. Or you can water
about once a month. When the last frost of spring has passed, you can place them
outside in a sunny spot or plant them directly in the ground. You will need to
prune and pinch plants until mid-July.

## Fall Planting

1. Select plants that have not bloomed yet.
2. Plant as soon as possible
3. Select a sunny location
4. Create a hole 2x larger than the circumference & 2x deeper than the pot.
5. Add compost to the soil before returning it to the hole
6. Set mums 18-24 inches apart
7. The crown should be right at or slightly above ground level
8. Backfill & press firmly to eliminate air pockets
9. Water in thoroughly
10. Mulch with shredded bark, leaves or straw a height of 2 inches except near
    the crown
11. After the first hard frost (<30 degrees F), add 2 more inches of mulch

## Pruning and Pinching

Pinching off the tip makes the plant branch out, leading each branch to grow its
own flower buds. Pinched mums offer many more blooms than those left alone. If
you don't like the leggy look, remove the tips of stems early in the season to
encourage branching. Repeat the process every two weeks until mid-July.
Winterize your in-ground mums by keeping them unpruned. For more protection, add
four to six inches of mulch after the ground freezes over.
