An hour outdoors before the day starts, in the grass between the beds, with the birds making rather more noise than anyone expects.

## What a session is like

Gentle, slow and led at a pace anyone can hold. Sessions move with the season — longer and slower in high summer, shorter and warmer as autumn comes in. No experience is needed, and nobody is watching how you do.

## What to bring

- A mat, a towel or a blanket
- Water
- Layers — mornings here are cooler than the forecast suggests

## Getting there

Come in through the main entrance and follow the path past the greenhouse. If it rains hard we move under cover rather than cancelling.

This series is offered on a pay-what-you-can basis. Nobody is turned away, and nobody is asked about it.
