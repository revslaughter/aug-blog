The greenhouse is full and the tables are out. Come find us on **44th Street**
any Saturday through May, or _any_ weekday if you call ahead.

## What we have this year

Everything below is grown on site, from seed, in soil we made ourselves.

- Tomatoes — Cherokee Purple, Sungold, and a paste variety we are still naming
- Peppers, sweet and hot
- Herbs by the flat: basil, dill, cilantro, three kinds of mint
- Mediterranean grapes and figs, for the ambitious

## Getting here

We are [north of the river](https://www.antiochurbangrowers.com/contact), which
confuses map applications more than it should. If your directions send you to a
cul-de-sac, you have gone one street too far — turn around and look for the
hoop houses.

1. Park on the street, not the gravel
2. Walk in past the ducks
3. Ask anyone holding a trowel

See you Saturday.
