---
title: Roasted Roots With Herb Oil
author: Maggie Samborski
---

What to do with the bottom of the winter share, when the carrots have gone
bendy and the turnips look like they know something.

## Ingredients

### For the roots

- [ ] Carrots, 500g, halved lengthways
- [ ] Turnips, 300g, in wedges
- [ ] Beets, 300g, peeled and quartered
- [ ] Olive oil, 40g
- [ ] Salt, more than feels correct

### For the herb oil

- [ ] Parsley, one large bunch
- [ ] Garlic, 2 cloves
- [ ] Olive oil, 100g
- [ ] Lemon, zest and juice

## Instructions

### Roast

1. Heat the oven as high as it will go
2. Toss the roots in oil and salt
3. Spread them out — crowd the pan and they steam
4. Roast 35 minutes, turning once, until the edges catch

### Blend

1. Blanch the parsley for ten seconds, then into cold water
2. Squeeze it dry, properly dry
3. Blend with the garlic, oil, zest and juice

### Serve forth

1. Spoon the oil over the hot roots
2. Eat standing at the counter, which is traditional
