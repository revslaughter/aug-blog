The Faire is the Produce Sale with everything else added on top: neighbours,
music, food, and a good excuse to spend a morning walking around the farm.

## What happens

- **Makers and growers** — produce, preserves, plants, and work by people from
  the neighbourhood
- **Food** — something cooking, usually with what came out of the ground that week
- **Music** — local players through the morning
- **Farm tours** — a walk through the beds, the compost windrows and the animals

## Bringing something

Wanting a table for your own growing or making? Get in touch and we will find
you a spot. Priority goes to people growing or making within a few miles of here.

Parking is on the street, and the whole site is walkable from the entrance.
Children and leashed dogs are welcome — the geese have opinions about both, but
they keep them to themselves.
